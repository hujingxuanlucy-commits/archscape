The idea

See the city
through your own lens.

ArchScape is built around one loop: discover a place, capture it, add it to your journey, and let that journey create new connections. Architecture becomes something you notice while moving through the city — not something you look up later.

Map grammar
Nearby POI Saved Visited Current Journey Up next Friend Explorer
One map language, three grammars: category on Explore, status on Journey, person on Path Crossings. Hue carries one meaning per screen.
Image language · architecture remains the hero
Use real architectural photography with strong geometry, material, light, and spatial depth. UI should frame the image, not compete with it. Final showcase screenshots will be linked from showcase-assets/ rather than redrawn.
Design principles
Editorial firstTypography and photography create the character; controls stay quiet.
Spatially preciseMaps, routes, markers, and distance are part of the product language.
Color has a job#952324 signals identity, selection, route state, or action — never generic decoration.
Real over renderedShow actual UI exports and actual architectural imagery. Avoid reconstructed screenshots when final assets exist.
